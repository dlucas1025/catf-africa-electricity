# CATF Africa — Electricity Sector Analysis

> Partnered with the Clean Air Task Force (CATF) Africa to explore country-level electricity-sector indicators across 50+ African countries, generating insights to inform the design of their Electricity Sector Performance Scorecard (CATF-ESPS).

## Course context

University of Toronto STA130 - Winter 2026, group of 4 students.

## My contribution

I owned one of four interconnected research questions, focusing on **electricity reliability** measured via SAIDI (System Average Interruption Duration Index). Found that total annual outage time is a direct indicator of reliability and varies sharply across African regions.

## Stack

R (dplyr, ggplot2, kable) · Quarto (poster output)

## Deliverables

- `analysis.qmd` — my portion of the analysis (R code and visualizations)
- `poster.pdf` — final group poster, presented at the UofT STA130 Poster Fair, March 2026
